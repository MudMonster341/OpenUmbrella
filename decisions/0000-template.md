# NNNN — <decision, stated as a decision>

## Context
The forces at play. What made this a decision rather than a default: the constraint, the
disagreement, or the thing that would be expensive to reverse. Include the numbers that mattered.

## Options considered
1. **<Option A>** — what it is, and its concrete cost or risk.
2. **<Option B>** — same.
3. **<Option C>** — same.

Only options actually weighed. Inventing alternatives after the fact makes the record worse than
silence.

## Decision
One sentence, active voice. Then the argument, in terms of the forces named in Context.

## Consequences
- What this makes easy.
- What this makes hard, or gives up. **An ADR with no downside listed has not been thought through.**
- The tripwire that should trigger a superseding ADR.
